## Scene Style

All gameplay images: **Pixel art, side view, 16-bit retro game art style, clean black outlines, vibrant colors. Wooden arm wrestling table. Festive birthday party background, balloons and colorful banners. Medium wide shot, waist-up framing. Warm overhead party lighting, colorful ambient glow. Both players always use their right hand — right hands gripped together as one single locked unit — they move as one, never separately. No text, no UI elements.**

## Arm Position Key (side view)

In side view the right hands clasped are always horizontally centered between the two players. The action happens in depth:
- **Neutral**: hands raised high at center
- **Left player winning**: hands lowering toward the back of the table (away from camera)
- **Left player dominating**: hands pushed to the far back of the table, nearly touching the surface
- **Right player winning**: hands lowering toward the front of the table (toward the camera/viewer)
- **Right player dominating**: hands pushed to the near front of the table (toward viewer), nearly touching the surface
